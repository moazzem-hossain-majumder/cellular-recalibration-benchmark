# Reviewer recommendations: implementation and remaining evidence

## 1. Narrow the central claim — completed

The main manuscript is now **When Limited Recalibration Fails: Accounting for Cellular Throughput Commitments**. It states an empirical acquisition/decision contribution and rejects claims of optimal control, guaranteed positive-conditional risk, uniform superiority and live application benefit. Its three findings concern acquisition versus usable information, abstention versus conditional risk, and cohort-dependent trade-offs. The abstract's historical numerical claims remain independently bound to their saved ledgers.

## 2. Explain the failures — completed retrospectively

Reconstructed score windows and lower bounds for all 1,008 information-clock/window configurations. Saved threshold provenance, conservative-missing-score ties, zero bounds, pattern-gate vetoes and score ages. Decomposed all 1,848 earlier contrasts into shared-offer sizing, added service and removed service, with exact arithmetic reconciliation. Added 720 baseline decompositions. Verizon's W20 conservative-score suppression is specifically supported, while E3 and W40 counterexamples prevent attributing all failures to that mechanism. These diagnostics are descriptive; they do not identify a physical causal effect of radio changes.

## 3. Independent validation — preparation advanced; actual evidence outstanding

Prepared the adaptive-video shadow and live-trial protocols, explicit missing-outcome/stopping rules, cluster-risk precision sensitivity and machine-readable readiness report. Recreated the original 57-row Rome predictor, verified 877 archived Rome predictions (maximum absolute difference approximately 3.6e-15), serialized it, checked round-trip predictions and recorded its SHA-256. Selected and exported ten earlier E1 calibration observations as a proposed shared initialization. This is a reconstruction of the existing model, not training a new competitor or updating test forecasts.

No independent trips or live playback measurements have been collected. Operator, location, compatible device/logger, permitted endpoint, field feature compatibility, application requirements and registration remain unverified. Those are necessary physical and scientific prerequisites, not work that can be honestly completed by rewriting the manuscript. The archived-model export does not establish field compatibility. A final initial-state choice must be fixed before prospective data collection.

## 4. Targeted baselines — completed

Ran and validated 792 configurations including three random-priority query schedules, a published-update-inspired DecayingOGD adaptation, pooled calibration with the gate held fixed, and a separate gate removal. Preserved every seed, cohort and unequal-cost result. Random priorities use a known full eligible roster and are offline comparators; the prospective random schedule must be fixed over clock ticks before departure. The newer update is adapted to signed scores and delayed/missing feedback, so the publication's guarantee is not inherited.

Pattern quantiles provide no utility/risk change in the 72 fixed-gate comparisons. DecayingOGD versus Periodic has 9 utility gains without higher conditional upper risk, 18 losses without lower risk, and 45 trade-offs/risk-only changes. These counts are dependent settings, not independent replications. No default was replaced based on exploratory test performance.

## 5. Define practical usefulness — proposed profile and calculations completed; acceptance requirements unvalidated

Specified a transparent video-screening profile: 1 Mbps representation, 1.5 Mbps commitment threshold, 80% useful-service frequency, 5% maximum positive-conditional risk, proposed one-percentage-point risk noninferiority margin and five-percentage-point utility gain at no higher cost. These are engineering assumptions, not claimed user requirements, a stall-risk equivalence or confirmed feasible thresholds. The user delegated the choice of a useful application but did not supply measurements or accept specific operational thresholds. They must be validated before registration and cannot be applied retroactively as confirmatory criteria.

The utility precision grid and the clustered risk grid explicitly show why eighteen trips are not a universal sample size. A roughly 62-trip risk-precision scenario assumes 80 positive offers/trip, 0.20 intratrip correlation, 5% risk and a 2.5-point half-width. It is approximate and does not certify a 5% bound. No invented power claim is made.

## 6. Simplify the manuscript — completed for a generic IEEE journal-style draft

The main manuscript is six two-column pages, including the verified thirty-paper Related Work and a controller table. A separate fifteen-page supplementary PDF preserves the earlier detailed analyses and thirteen remaining numerical tables. All fourteen generated numerical tables remain checked across the two PDFs, their LaTeX sources, HTML and Markdown. The submission bundle contains both documents and the supporting protocols/results. Venue-specific template, page charges, anonymization and submission requirements remain to be checked after venue selection; no IEEE acceptance or submission is asserted.

## Recommendation

Major revision remains the honest recommendation. The retrospective mechanism evidence, targeted controls and presentation are stronger. The limiting evidence is now clearly identified: independently collected prospective results and validated application requirements, followed by a separate live randomized trial if QoE claims are retained. Further broad tuning of the inspected cohorts would not fill those gaps.
