# Independent review of the cellular recalibration benchmark

**Reviewer 3, statistics and reproducibility.** I would score the empirical benchmark **7.4/10** for submission to an applied measurement or reproducibility venue. This is a reviewer judgment, with no probability-of-acceptance interpretation. The strongest contribution is an auditable comparison of how much measured throughput a lower bound supports, how often it fails, and how the answer changes when logged targets disappear. The E3 example makes that distinction operationally important. Only three of 171 issued tree forecasts committed a positive rate, and two failed. [Forecast ledger](../results/clock_histgbm/issued_k10.csv)

## Findings affecting editorial judgment

| Severity | Location and finding | Consequence and remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Major | Rome experimental design, seven suffix dates per clock regime, six dates in common, fixed source fit. [Paired date ledger](../results/clock_histgbm/per_date.csv) | Confine inference to the sampled campaigns. Calendar-deletion ranges quantify sensitivity. Independent prospective deployments are needed for a population claim. |
| Major | E3 has 2/152 measured failures yet only 3/171 positive commitments. On E1 the same tree has 67/211 measured failures. [Statistical audit](STATISTICAL_VALIDITY_AUDIT.md) | Present failures, positive issuance and utility together in the abstract or main result. A reader considering admission control needs the risk when the system actually promises service. |
| Major | The original raw session endpoint and fixed-clock next-minute endpoint differ. Trace termination makes 45 of the 46 unavailable targets across both clock regimes physically ambiguous. [Clock construction](CLOCK_TRIGGERED_PRIMARY_MODEL_AUDIT.md) | Name each endpoint and identify target availability explicitly. Restrict sharp bounds to a latent nonnegative endpoint and identify a separate service semantics before interpreting post-termination throughput. |
| Major | The complete prefix frontier uses outcomes on the evaluation suffix. Global and grouped offsets coincide in the ten-label examples. [Frontier](../results/clock_histgbm/frontier.csv) | Retain fixed-budget results, label post-evaluation envelopes as exploratory, and externally test a locked selection rule before claiming an optimal budget or a general grouping gain. |
| Major for algorithmic novelty | The point predictor and calibration components are established procedures. No matched head-to-head against a published system on identical forecasts, labels, chronology and calibration budget is established by the Rome results. [Contribution review](NOVELTY_AND_CONTRIBUTION_AUDIT.md) | Position the paper as an empirical benchmark. A state-of-the-art claim needs a reproducible matched external baseline and a declared trade-off criterion. |
| Moderate | The 30 Irish random splits reuse 83 traces. The chronological 16-date check gives identical K = 10 global and grouped V of 34.81%. The Norwegian bus/metro transfer involves one legacy network. [Irish results](../results/external_validation/irish_group_validation.json) · [Metro transfer](../results/external_validation/metro_transfer.csv) | Report chronological outcomes, overlapping units and measurement technologies. Treat cross-context failures as evidence of limits within each protocol. |
| Moderate | The baseline manifest predates the audited commit. On a fresh Windows checkout, 52 of 216 recorded raw-byte hashes differed, including 34 explained by uniform LF normalization. The other 18 did not match either uniform newline convention. [Manifest generator](../scripts/generate_experiment_manifest.py) | Version the hash convention, check canonical text and raw binary hashes on a fresh checkout, and keep exact revisions with the hosted verification. The residual 18 discrepancies have no established common cause. |
| Minor | V on measured ticks, failure across all issued ticks and conditional failure on positives use different denominators. Comparing percentages alone can obscure differences of practical importance. [Per-date counts](../results/clock_histgbm/per_date.csv) | State counts alongside percentages and distinguish identification ranges from sampling confidence intervals in table headings and captions. |

## Publication risk

A reviewer seeking a new forecasting algorithm or a universally reliable bound has grounds to reject this contribution on scope. A measurement reviewer can assess the benchmark through its paired deployment-style forecasts, explicit handling of unobserved endpoints, negative external results and reproducible ledgers. Keep claims at that level. The priority for a stronger subsequent paper is to freeze the predictor, budget, endpoint and utility criterion before gathering an independent time block or operator, and to collect enough positive commitments to estimate service risk with meaningful precision.
