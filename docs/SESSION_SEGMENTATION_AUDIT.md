# What the 2023 throughput endpoints measure

The retained method is **Option B**. Active-start segmentation defines a different observational unit. We retain its adverse results in the paper and diagnostics. The Rome archive supplies no independent truth label for these boundaries.

Each file and operator is processed separately in recorded order. Parse Day and fractional time with the explicit day.month.year format. Convert the Mean Netw. DL column to Mbps according to its unit, coerce nonnumeric values (including ?) to missing, and drop missing and negative throughput before differencing. Zero is retained. Reset begins on a change below −5 Mbps to a value below 5. Relative drop begins below −2 Mbps to a value below 1. Despite the name, the latter threshold uses an absolute change. Burst begins on every crossing from at most 1 to above 1 Mbps or any consecutive retained-row gap over 10 seconds. It keeps inactive rows and can retain an initial inactive segment. The final retained value, including zero, is the target. Elapsed duration is last minus first timestamp.

Sensitivity rates use all eligible target pairs. Sort segments within trace/operator by start and use the preceding completed segment if lag age is 0–60 seconds. A separate source-trained HistGBM and frozen source offset evaluate both later operators. The primary benchmark instead evaluates fixed chronological test suffixes. The reset trigger reads throughput at the inferred start. Even with strictly earlier predictor fields, an online forecaster would require an external start signal to know when to issue a prediction. Adding segments changes eligible target dates' weights, lagged features, calibration prefix composition and endpoints. More neighboring segments do not imply a larger independent sample.

* In Location_2_IS_Ookla_TV_TTI_1.csv, Op2 reset segment 2 spans 1,710.999 s across 3,393 readings. The largest retained-row gap is 1.002 s. Its 34 upward crossings show that continuous logging can encompass repeated bursts without triggering reset. Either multiple tests or activity within one test could account for the trace. Of 30 reset segments longer than 180 s, duration alone cannot resolve which were merged. The same file contains the only reset segment with an internal gap over 10 s (Op1, segment 29, 16.999 s). Relative drop also bridges one such gap. Burst bridges none.
* Location_11_IS_Ookla_TV_TTI_3.csv, Op1 reset segment 4, contains one row at 0.001 Mbps. All three zero-duration resets should be treated as endpoints with no measured span. No independent completion evidence establishes that they denote full downloads.
* Location_1_IS_Ookla_TV_TTI_0.csv, Op1 burst segment 1, starts at 2.6 Mbps, reaches 275.1 and ends at exactly zero after 83.002 seconds. Zero here is a recorded numeric value, distinct from the missing sentinel. The trace cannot distinguish every true stall, idle tail or logging convention. Across burst units 822 endpoints are below 1 Mbps and 183 are zero. The median target falls from 19.6478 Mbps under reset to 0.1 Mbps. In 131 reset segments there are at least two internal upward crossings. This establishes fragmentation relative to reset units, without establishing the number of actual tests.

The three segments_*.csv files contain every audited trace/operator ID, timestamps, counts, durations, peaks, endpoints, internal upward crossings and maximum gap. session_boundary_sensitivity.json also gives duration bins, zero and low counts, causal denominators, prediction errors and supported fractions for every method. session_segmentation_comparison.json records per-file counts. Both reset-based rules miss some drops, depend on the filter and have different rates. Ground-truth test identifiers or independently synchronized completion logs would be needed to validate either as a physical session.

The raw filter audit counts 500,606 timestamped rows viewed for two operators (1,001,212 operator-row slots). Of those slots 897,113 have missing or nonnumeric throughput, none is negative, 104,099 are retained and 14,638 are numeric zero. All retained timestamps parse and increase strictly within their trace/operator. No raw gap exceeds ten seconds. Filtering creates one such gap. The trace_filters.csv inventory records the counts for each of 86 series.

Restricting scoring to positive endpoints, with the original segments and fitted model, gives E1/E3 violation rates of 0.1333/0.2293 (reset, N=270/205), 0.1180/0.1993 (relative drop, N=322/281), and 0.3742/0.4651 (burst, N=318/372). Retaining zeros is the primary analysis. This conditional diagnostic changes the population and cannot determine whether an individual zero is an outage.
