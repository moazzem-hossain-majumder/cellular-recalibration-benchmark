# IEEE reviewer response and critical assessment

These are author-side reviewer perspectives. They are not external IEEE reviews or acceptance predictions. The baseline audit used separate review agents; their final turns were interrupted by a usage limit. The final controller assessment below was completed by the primary agent using the generated ledgers, validator, isolated rerun and literature comparison.

## Reviewer 1 — wireless communications and networking

The contribution has a direct service interpretation and now accounts for scheduled no-lag opportunities. The two Rome and four external LTE/5G cohorts use the same frozen predictors and explicit endpoint mappings. The principal weakness is that MARUR fails to improve the cost-risk-utility trade-off consistently. Extra requests explain part of the E1 gain, and US service gains often accompany increased violations. Patterns in recorded RSRP do not identify a physical radio state or outage. Three carriers on one US drive are correlated exposure. A live controller/application trial remains absent.

Response implemented: Table XIV reports actual costs and positive-conditional bounds; Figure 8 shows all predefined caps. Frozen, periodic, early and adaptive comparators share point forecasts and initial labels. All external adverse outcomes remain in the paper. The contribution is an empirical acquisition-and-decision framework. Required future evidence is a frozen policy on independently scheduled trips with actual application outcomes.

Scores out of ten: novelty 6.5, technical contribution 7.0, mathematical formulation 7.0, experimental design 8.0, statistical validity 7.0, external validity 7.0, reproducibility 8.5, manuscript quality 7.0. Recommendation: Major Revision for a venue expecting a novel adaptive algorithm. A measurement-oriented venue could consider the scoped empirical contribution.

## Reviewer 2 — machine learning and statistical methodology

The signed-residual upper completion for missing requested outcomes is auditable under nonnegative latent throughput. Query selection precedes label access, includes delayed feedback, and consumes budget even when a response is unavailable. The method does not solve its constrained objective with a guarantee. Its support gate and time trigger are chosen heuristics. Prior work already treats selective prediction, missing inputs, adaptive conformal calibration and costly label stopping. The archival test suffixes have been inspected previously, so this is exploratory method development.

Response implemented: mathematical assumptions and unsupported guarantees are explicit; a nominal-level ACI adaptation is distinguished from the old offset-feedback baseline. The eight-policy controller family has no test-based winner selection. The point-error metrics remain identical across policies. Missing-response, pattern and schedule ablations expose component effects. Date contrasts are descriptive and carry no tick-level significance claim. Further data or stronger theory would be needed for population reliability or algorithmic superiority.

Scores out of ten: novelty 6.0, technical contribution 7.0, mathematical formulation 7.5, experimental design 7.5, statistical validity 7.0, external validity 7.0, reproducibility 8.5, manuscript quality 7.0. Recommendation: Major Revision for an algorithm-focused IEEE paper. The useful current result is a falsifiable budgeted evaluation with clear negative findings.

## Reviewer 3 — reproducibility and experimental validity

The isolated baseline pipeline executed 29 stages and all 71 baseline tests. A later isolated run reproduced the six deterministic MARUR data files byte for byte and all 576 scientific summaries, excluding machine timing. The updated tests exercise production code, and an independent validator checks 160,032 actions and 5,960 responses. Package discovery now resolves src imports outside the checkout. The code's previously asserted Apache license lacked a LICENSE file and was removed; the author must choose a release license. The raw Norwegian and Irish data terms need to remain distinct from source-code licensing.

Response implemented: execution receipts preserve scope, dates, hashes and successful commands. The baseline full pipeline and later MARUR run are documented separately. All data sources are acquired under pinned manifests, and submission materials contain no raw archives. No current hosted CI result or fresh operating-system provisioning is claimed. The historical CS-CP audit now identifies Protocol B's transductive test-covariate access.

Scores out of ten: novelty 6.5, technical contribution 7.5, mathematical formulation 7.5, experimental design 8.0, statistical validity 7.0, external validity 7.0, reproducibility 8.5, manuscript quality 7.0. Recommendation: Major Revision before a release claiming comprehensive IEEE readiness. Venue selection, author license choice and a reviewer focused on the target venue remain unresolved.

## Consolidated response

The implementation materially strengthens the experimental question and exposes the consequences of unsuccessful calibration requests. It does not establish that MARUR is a superior or formally risk-controlled adaptive method. Present the paper as a study of budgeted commitment decisions under incomplete feedback. Keep the negative controller results and explicit cost model. The next experiment should freeze policies before collecting a new campaign. A 9/10 claim would exceed the current evidence.
