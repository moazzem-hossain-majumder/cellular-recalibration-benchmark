# Conditions that break a ten-label commitment

The clock stress cells reuse observed records and offsets unless a different calibration source or horizon is explicitly stated. Subgroups defined by the future drop or zero outcome are retrospective error diagnostics. stress.csv provides denominators and both rates. No simulation is included.

- **Severe drop and zero.** Among 53 E1 ticks with observed falls of at least 10 Mbps the frozen global tree violated 50.94%. Among 30 zero endpoints it violated 56.67%. U is undefined on an all-zero outcome stratum. E3 had 43 severe drops and 4.65% violations there.
- **Radio availability.** E1 measured tree violations were 10.83% without NR and 59.34% on dual-radio ticks. A fixed threshold policy emitting zero when NR is absent reduced aggregate E1 V from 31.75 to 25.59% but reduced U to 6.16%. E3 remained at 1.32% V and negligible U. The indicator cannot identify why radio values are missing.
- **Sparse or shifted calibration.** Every feasible prefix is scored. Using the other operator's first ten available labels changed tree V to 1.90% in E1 and 32.89% in E3. The source-only values were 34.60% and 36.84%. Date changes and an online update are reported in the statistical audit. Physical regime changes and recovery times have no ground truth here.
- **Short/long observations.** For E1, seven ticks had at most ten seconds of recorded prior rate span, with 14.29% tree violations; 211 had at least fifty seconds, with 29.79% violations on 188 scored. These are window spans. The separate session segmentation audit documents true changes to the inferred short/long session population, including single-row resets and a reset spanning over 1,710 seconds.
- **Unavailable targets.** For the E3 tree the 19 unavailable outcomes all had zero commitment. Its all-issued rate is identified despite missing outcomes. E1 has eight unresolved positive commitments; even its lower risk endpoint exceeds 10%. The ledger distinguishes trace termination and measurement unavailability collectively; a physical outage cannot be inferred.
- **Different horizon.** Keep the issue tick and inputs fixed and recalibrate on the first ten measured second-minute outcomes. E1 tree V/U became 4.35/0.35% on 184 measured targets and E3 1.50/0.08% on 133. The changed offset and measurable population limit any horizon-only interpretation.
- **Alternative definitions.** The original reset, relative-drop and burst analyses remain reported, including adverse changes to session counts and endpoints. Forecasting a clock-window endpoint does not recover a physical test boundary.
