# Final adversarial peer review

These reviews precede the [three-carrier Drive 3 extension](DRIVE3_REPLICATION.md). The [current scorecard](FINAL_SCORECARD.md) includes that evidence.

Three separate author-side review passes assessed the same project from different disciplines. These scores concern the empirical measurement paper. No external editor or IEEE reviewer participated.

## Reviewer 1 — telecommunications, 7.0/10

The fixed-clock replay and the US publisher's transfer roster let readers check when forecasts were actually possible. The operator could use positive-offer frequency alongside failure rate when evaluating a proposed minimum sending rate. Rome's outcome-dependent session boundaries, overlapping measurement dates and the single informative US test date limit deployment implications. A smaller presentation concern is the distinction between a scheduled date and a date that supplied forecasts. Report all three US abstentions, eight positive offers and six violations. Obtain multiple independently instrumented dates before claiming cross-carrier reliability. Incremental methodological novelty and a generalization claim would be major rejection risks at a selective journal.

## Reviewer 2 — machine learning, 7.0/10

Matched persistence, chronological calibration and the decision ledger make failures interpretable. Synthetic input loss holds the evaluation rows fixed. The tree was trained on 57 sessions and produces either excessive violations or negligible supported throughput in the headline comparison. The burst model has eight calibration runs, the periodic intervention fixes one phase, and the random seeds quantify only simulated variation. Keep the adverse results and distinguish measured utility from risk across all issued forecasts. An independent multi-date replay with the same telemetry and endpoint is needed to establish performance beyond these campaigns. A venue expecting an algorithm that improves on existing predictors is a poor fit.

## Reviewer 3 — research methodology, 7.4/10

The paired ledger, sharp finite-record bounds and reproducible negative examples support an applied measurement submission. Six of the seven suffix dates per operator overlap. All three positive E3 tree offers and both failures occurred on June 10. Date-deletion results are sensitivity diagnostics. The many retrospective choices of budget give ample opportunity for favorable selection. Clarify feedback costs, retain the preselected ten-label result, and require a new locked evaluation for any superiority claim. Date counts, the scope of clean checks, and the historical nature of hosted CI need explicit labels. Misstating either precision or reproducibility could motivate rejection.

## Consolidated judgment

The unweighted reviewer mean is 7.13/10. A specialized conference with a cellular measurement or reproducibility track is plausible. The principal contribution is the demonstration that counting all forecasts can make a system look reliable even when it rarely offers a positive rate and frequently breaks those positive commitments. Retain the observed-utility and conditional-failure denominators and the negative US and selector results. Prospective replication is the most important remaining revision for a selective journal.
