# Minimum prospective validation protocol

Status: proposed and unexecuted. Freeze and register this protocol, the selected operator/location/routes, source model hash, initial residual IDs, feature mapping, software revision and analysis script before any new outcome is inspected. The smallest useful study tests replication of the empirical trade-off; it cannot establish a general algorithmic theorem or live application benefit.

## Precision justification and number of independent trips

Primary estimand: the equally weighted mean paired trip difference in observed supported-throughput fraction, MARUR minus Periodic, at the same prespecified NEW-query cap. Each independently scheduled trip contributes one pair. Treat ticks and simultaneous carriers within a trip as dependent.

Planning assumption: SD of paired trip differences is 0.10 (ten percentage points). This is an explicit planning assumption, not an estimate justified by the small existing archive. Target a two-sided 95% interval half-width of 0.05. Solve t_(0.975,n-1)*0.10/sqrt(n)<=0.05: the minimum is 18 completed independent trips. Schedule 22 to allow approximately 15% pre-departure cancellation/instrumentation attrition (ceil(18/0.85)); this does not guarantee 18 completions. Include every started trip, including failures. Never discard a trip because it has poor service, missing outcomes or no positive offers.

The precision is modest and does not resolve small effects. With SD 0.15 the same target needs 38 complete trips (45 scheduled); with SD 0.20 it needs 64 (76 scheduled). At SD 0.10 and a tighter 2.5-percentage-point half-width, 64 complete trips are required. scripts/audit_marur.py writes all assumptions and calculations to results/marur_audit/prospective_precision.csv. These are approximate t-based precision calculations assuming independent trip differences and a sufficiently regular sampling distribution. They are not power calculations or a guarantee of conditional-risk certification.

Use one separately scheduled drive on each of 18 different dates, with route/time strata fixed in advance and no subdivision of a continuous drive into independent units. A single operator/device configuration is the minimum scope. Multiple carriers collected concurrently remain one campaign cluster. Replication across independent operators or cities needs additional campaigns and a revised precision calculation. If weather, route repetition or shared events create cross-date dependence, report it and increase uncertainty; merely reaching 18 dates does not prove independence.

## Measurement and frozen policies

Use 120-minute drives with synchronized monotonic and UTC clocks. Choose a route/time mixture before collection and retain all scheduled ticks. Issue forecasts on a fixed one-minute clock, never on a throughput change or successful download. Use a fixed full next-60-second outcome: delivered application payload bits divided by 60 seconds. Document instrumentation overhead and avoid changing the measurement workload by forecast policy.

Freeze the existing Rome HistGBM predictor and exact input preprocessing; no refitting or hyperparameter selection on new trips. Validate feature mappings using an outcome-blinded engineering rehearsal. Predeclare a specific archived ten-successful-label initialization and its residual/feature IDs; reset every policy to that same state at each trip start. Its historical acquisition attempts remain unknown and separate from new trial cost. If this initialization is unsuitable, changing it is a protocol amendment before collection, not a retrospective adjustment.

Freeze MARUR exactly as audited: FIFO window 10, alpha 0.1, three-entry pattern gate, nine-entry grouping threshold, sixty-minute completed-response age, one pending request and conservative missing score p. Primary new-query cap is 10 per trip. Baselines are Periodic (every fifteenth eligible opportunity), Early, the audited modified ACI, and Frozen. Frozen makes zero new requests. All use the same predictor, opportunity roster and initial state. No policy gets another policy's calibration label unless it independently requested that opportunity.

The primary comparison has an equal cap, not guaranteed equal realized cost. Report actual costs and service jointly; superiority cannot be claimed from utility alone when cost differs. Predeclared secondary replay caps 0,1,5,10 report exact-cost contrasts only when realized counts match, preserving the full trip suffix. Do not force extra MARUR requests, waste measurements to charge Frozen, interpolate costs, or change the schedule to manufacture equality. A failure to reach a cost is itself a result.

## Observation and missingness

Keep the evaluator's complete scheduled measurement log separate from each controller's feedback view. A query is charged at issue and released at its prespecified 60-second deadline. Record issue, deadline, completion status, payload, availability flags, admission status, and whether the update was available to a later forecast. Log latest attempt, completed response, admitted score and successful label separately. Charge evaluator measurements, ordinary lag measurements, bytes and energy separately from policy feedback queries; simulated feedback cost is not physical measurement savings.

If logging reliably records zero delivered payload over a complete test, y=0 is observed. If the outcome cannot be reconstructed, y is missing, not zero or successful service. Missing requested labels consume a query and insert the conservative score p. Unrequested or failed feedback remains unavailable to the controller even when the evaluator recovered it. Distinguish missing predictors, feedback loss and missing audit outcomes. No synthetic censoring is needed for the primary prospective trial; any stress scenario must be declared secondary and cannot replace natural outcomes.

For missing positive audit outcomes, report both possible failure endpoints. For no-lag opportunities, record ineligibility/abstention and retain the scheduled denominator. If an entire started trip has no measurable throughput, observed utility is undefined: report it explicitly, retain its risk/accounting contribution, and state that observed-only utility inference is selected. Do not replace it silently with a clean trip. Missingness can invalidate the intended precision or interpretation.

## Endpoints and statistical analysis

For each policy and trip report measured supported throughput, scheduled and eligible counts, positive frequency on both denominators, abstention, measured-positive failures, finite-record all-issued and positive-conditional risk bounds, and positive-date/trip counts. Report initial observations, new attempts, successes, failures, admissions and effective updates separately. Summarize both equally weighted trip metrics and pooled ledger metrics, labeling their different estimands. Do not average conditional risks over only positive trips without reporting zero-positive trips.

The single primary numerical comparison is paired mean utility difference with a two-sided 95% t interval across independent trips and a trip-cluster bootstrap sensitivity analysis (10,000 draws, seed 20261121). Risk and actual query cost are mandatory joint interpretation criteria. For pooled conditional risk, bootstrap whole paired trips and recompute numerator/denominator and missing-outcome endpoints. Report undefined bootstrap draws if no positive offers occur; such evidence is insufficient for certification. Bootstrap endpoints add sampling uncertainty to missing-data bounds; the raw bounds alone are not confidence intervals.

Do not declare useful risk-controlled superiority unless a prespecified operational service threshold is met, uncertainty supports benefit, risk is acceptable, and actual cost is no greater. The operator must specify the minimum useful service fraction before registration; without that value the experiment estimates a trade-off, not operational feasibility. A one-sided 95% upper sampling bound on worst-case positive-conditional risk below 0.10 would support that narrowly specified population claim only under the sampling assumptions. Eighteen trips may not achieve it. The study's fixed sample target is for utility precision, not universal 10% risk certification.

All other baselines, caps and subgroups are secondary descriptive results; any formal multiple comparisons use Holm adjustment over the declared family. No early stopping for favorable outcomes, no post-hoc winning policy, no tick-level independent confidence intervals. If realized SD is higher than assumed, report the wider interval and plan a separately registered extension. Release anonymized ledgers and hashes subject to dataset permissions. A subsequent randomized live application study is needed for claims about playback stalls or deployment benefit; shadow replay does not provide those claims.
