Editorial Office, IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management

Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, When Limited Recalibration
Fails: Accounting for Cellular Throughput Commitments, formatted in
two-column IEEEtran journal style for consideration as an empirical
measurement and evaluation-methodology contribution in IEEE Transactions
on Network and Service Management (with IEEE Open Journal of the
Communications Society as an appropriate open-access companion venue).
This study distinguishes measurement attempts, usable feedback,
selective positive commitments, and physical collection-unit clusters
across archived commercial cellular traces (three geographic campaigns
encompassing sixteen distinct calendar dates). Matched-cost comparisons,
randomized query controls, isolated calibration/gating ablations, and
cluster-level inference expose when feedback improves commitments and
when unequal cost, Day-1 budget depletion, or commitment shrinkage
mislead. The main manuscript stays strictly focused on cellular
throughput commitments, explicitly deferring live application QoE trials
and prospective field collection to future work. Repository source code
is released under the MIT License, with third-party raw dataset terms
documented and excluded from redistribution. No optimal controller,
guaranteed conditional risk, live QoE claim, or prospective field trial
is asserted.

Sincerely,\
Md. Moazzem Hossain Majumder
