\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\subsection{Cellular Measurement, Prediction, and Rate Decisions}
Operational measurements establish that cellular throughput depends on radio conditions, mobility, and collection protocol. Earlier channel/context traces~\cite{raca2020} and commercial millimeter-wave (mmWave) fifth-generation (5G) campaigns~\cite{narayanan2020} enabled context-aware rate prediction, while newer multi-technology datasets and machine-learning evaluations extend these insights to non-standalone 5G deployments~\cite{kousias2024data,caso2024,kousias2024model}. However, forecast errors sampled within a single route or shared drive are temporally correlated rather than independent replications, and low point error does not bound rate-commitment violations.

Missing inputs and deployment realism motivate complementary lines of work. The Boosted Additive Model for data with Missing Observations (BAMMO) addresses missing input covariates in commercial 5G prediction~\cite{jacobson2025}, rather than failed acquisition of future calibration outcomes. Smartphone streaming traces expose real-time sampling shift~\cite{raca2024}, multi-carrier measurements reveal divergent link predictability~\cite{basit2024}, and \emph{CellReplay}~\cite{sentosa2025cellreplay} solves trace-replay emulation drift caused by adaptive workload feedback. Even without emulation-workload drift on fixed logs, however, global, grouped, and adaptive conformal baselines such as Sirhan~\cite{sirhan2026} evaluate full-outcome splits without auditing probe budgets or missing completions.

\subsection{Why Isolated Accounting Adjustments Are Insufficient}
\label{sec:related_gap}
Existing statistical methodologies examine missingness, adaptation, or sparse labels individually: missing-pattern conformal prediction~\cite{zaffran2023} and covariate-shift or weighted quantiles~\cite{tibshirani2019,barber2023} focus on covariate or smooth distributional drift; sequential time-series conformal methods~\cite{zaffran2022,xu2023,lin2022,gibbs2024}, ACI/OGD~\cite{gibbs2021,bhatnagar2023,pid2023,angelopoulos2024online,podkopaev2024}, and risk control~\cite{bates2021,angelopoulos2024risk,angelopoulos2021ltt,gibbs2025,gauthier2026} generally assume complete step-wise feedback; while upfront stopping~\cite{ilyas2026}, semi-bandit feedback~\cite{ge2025}, sporadic feedback~\cite{hou2025}, and selective abstention/stability analyses~\cite{geifman2017,min2026} treat isolated aspects of cost or abstention. On real cellular logs, however, evaluation failures compound across layers: shrinking offer frequency masks conditional risk spikes while simultaneously changing realized cooldown queries, position-locked missingness interacts asymmetrically with periodic updates, and offer-count covariance across heterogeneous cohorts flips pooled contrasts.
