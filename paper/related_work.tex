\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\subsection{Cellular Measurement, Prediction, and Rate Decisions}
Operational measurements establish that cellular throughput depends on radio conditions, mobility, and collection protocol. Earlier channel/context traces~\cite{raca2020} and commercial millimeter-wave (mmWave) fifth-generation (5G) campaigns~\cite{narayanan2020} enabled context-aware rate prediction, while newer multi-technology datasets and machine-learning evaluations extend these insights to non-standalone 5G deployments~\cite{kousias2024data,caso2024,kousias2024model}. However, forecast errors sampled within a single route or shared drive are temporally correlated rather than independent replications, and low point error does not bound rate-commitment violations.

Missing inputs and deployment realism motivate complementary lines of work. The Boosted Additive Model for data with Missing Observations (BAMMO) handles missing covariates during commercial 5G throughput modeling~\cite{jacobson2025}, addressing unobserved inputs rather than unsuccessful acquisition of future calibration outcomes. Smartphone video streaming studies highlight real-time sampling shift~\cite{raca2024}, multi-carrier experiments document conflicting rate histories across links~\cite{basit2024}, and \emph{CellReplay}~\cite{sentosa2025cellreplay} solves trace-replay emulation bias caused by workload-dependent cellular adaptation across multiple recorded workloads. Even on fixed recorded traces without replay-workload distortion, however, Sirhan~\cite{sirhan2026} and conformal baselines do not audit how unequal probe spending, missing horizon completions, offer-weighted pooling, and campaign clustering jointly flip controller evaluations.

\subsection{Missingness, Adaptation, and Budgeted Feedback}
Conformal calibration with missing covariates separates marginal coverage from missingness-pattern conditioning~\cite{zaffran2023}, whereas covariate-shift reweighting~\cite{tibshirani2019} and non-exchangeable weighted quantiles~\cite{barber2023} rely on structural weights that degrade under unmodeled drift and outcome censoring. Sequential time-series conformal methods~\cite{zaffran2022,xu2023,lin2022,gibbs2024}, Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI) and online variants~\cite{gibbs2021,bhatnagar2023,pid2023,angelopoulos2024online,podkopaev2024}, and risk-controlling formulations~\cite{bates2021,angelopoulos2024risk,angelopoulos2021ltt,gibbs2025,gauthier2026} assume complete label revelation after each step, while selective classification~\cite{geifman2017} and interval-stability critiques~\cite{min2026} formalize abstention--risk interactions.

Recent work examines partial or costly feedback directly: upfront stopping rules under i.i.d.\ arrivals~\cite{ilyas2026}, semi-bandit feedback revealed by set inclusion~\cite{ge2025}, and sporadic edge--cloud calibration~\cite{hou2025}. Our study complements these directions by auditing real cellular ledgers jointly over probe expenditures, unobserved outcomes, $R=(M/N)R_+$, and physical cluster equivalence (Table~I).
