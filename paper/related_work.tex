\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\subsection{Cellular Measurement, Prediction, and Rate Decisions}
Operational measurements establish that cellular throughput depends on radio conditions, mobility, and the collection regime. The channel/context dataset of Raca et al.~\cite{raca2020} and the commercial mmWave measurements underlying Lumos5G~\cite{narayanan2020} support context-aware prediction. More recent multi-technology measurements, longitudinal service analysis, and machine-learning modeling extend this evidence to evolving non-standalone networks~\cite{kousias2024data,caso2024,kousias2024model}. However, forecast counts within a shared route or campaign are temporally correlated rather than independent replications, and point error does not measure commitment violation risk.

Missing predictors and deployment constraints motivate complementary approaches. BAMMO incorporates missing covariates into additive boosting on commercial 5G data~\cite{jacobson2025}, addressing missing inputs rather than unsuccessful acquisition of future calibration outcomes. Real-time smartphone evaluations highlight sampling, labeling, and distribution shift~\cite{raca2024}, while multi-carrier experiments reveal conflicting histories that limit fine-grained accuracy~\cite{basit2024}. The closest work to our decision objective is Sirhan's evaluation of global, regime-conditioned, and adaptive conformal throughput intervals~\cite{sirhan2026}, which shows that mobility conditioning can aid two-sided intervals while a global one-sided bound performs competitively for rate selection. Neither work audits how unequal probe spending, unsuccessful responses, and heavy abstention interact across budgets and physical campaigns.

\subsection{Missingness, Adaptation, and Budgeted Feedback}
Conformal calibration under missing covariates distinguishes marginal coverage from missingness-pattern conditioning~\cite{zaffran2023}. Covariate-shift weighting~\cite{tibshirani2019} and non-exchangeable weighted conformal methods~\cite{barber2023} establish bounds under structural assumptions that break under unrestricted temporal drift and outcome censoring. Time-series conformal methods~\cite{zaffran2022,xu2023,lin2022,gibbs2024}, online level adaptation~\cite{gibbs2021,bhatnagar2023,pid2023,angelopoulos2024online,podkopaev2024}, and risk-controlling formulations~\cite{bates2021,angelopoulos2024risk,gibbs2025,gauthier2026} establish strong full-feedback baselines, while selective classification~\cite{geifman2017} and stability critiques~\cite{min2026} formalize the link between abstention and conditional error.

Budgeted and partial-feedback calibration has recently gained direct attention. Ilyas et al.~\cite{ilyas2026} analyze upfront stopping rules for i.i.d.\ arrivals, Ge et al.~\cite{ge2025} study stochastic semi-bandit feedback revealed by set inclusion, and Hou et al.~\cite{hou2025} calibrate probabilistic solvers via sporadic edge--cloud feedback. Our empirical contribution bridges these threads inside real cellular logs by accounting jointly for charged measurement probes, failed responses, selective offer frequency $R = (M/N)R_+$, and physical collection-date dependence across established reactive (MARUR) and paced (PACER) comparators.
